Bubble Fusion

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Summary

Bubble Fusion is an offline puzzle game. We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. The game does not require an account, and it works without an internet connection.

1. Who we are

Bubble Fusion (package name com.bubble.fusion) is developed by Epic Project (“we”, “us”, “our”). This policy explains how the app handles information when you play it on your Android device.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise or approximate location, advertising ID, or device identifiers.

The game saves your progress on your own device only. This includes things like your current level, stars, coins, boosters, daily-reward date and your sound, music and vibration settings. This data stays in the app’s local storage on your device, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Permissions

The app does not request any special permissions. It uses the vibration feature of your device (if you leave it enabled in Settings) to give haptic feedback during play. You can turn this off at any time from the Settings screen.

4. Third-party services, ads and analytics

This version of Bubble Fusion does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services, and does not share data with third parties. The game may load a web font from Google Fonts when an internet connection is available; Google may receive standard technical information (such as an IP address) as part of that request, as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

If we add ads, in-app purchases or online features in the future, we will update this policy before they are released and describe exactly what data is involved.

5. Children’s privacy

The game is suitable for a general audience. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country).

6. Data security and retention

Since all game data is stored locally on your device, it is protected by your device’s own security. We do not hold any of your data, so we have nothing to retain or delete on our side. To remove the saved data, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the game.

7. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct or delete. If you have any questions or requests about privacy, please contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the “Last updated” date above will change when we make updates.

9. Contact us
